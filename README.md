## About

I'm **KLOWZY** (also known as **Eng. Klowzy** and **KlowzyZ**) — a computer engineer and independent developer who designs, builds, and ships software.

My focus is on tools that solve real problems: diagnosing disks, finding devices on local networks, managing CCTV equipment, moving files, and making everyday Windows workflows easier. I care about clear interfaces, sensible safeguards, privacy, and software that works outside a demo.

**Areas I work in:** `C# / .NET` · `WPF / WinForms` · `Windows desktop` · `Networking` · `ONVIF / CCTV` · `Storage & diagnostics` · `Local-first utilities`
